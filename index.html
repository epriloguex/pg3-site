Strategy Overview

ProGold 3 AI is a hybrid human-AI gold trading model developed specifically for XAUUSD, combining experienced trader judgement with systematic market analysis and rule-based risk controls. It is operated by PG3 AI on the company’s own proprietary account.

01
AI-Powered Macro Synthesis
Real-time ingestion of central bank signals, inflation data, and USD correlations — executing when macro catalysts align with technical momentum.
02
Adaptive Momentum Architecture
Multi-timeframe signal layering with dynamic position sizing that scales conviction to realized volatility — not static lot sizes.
03
Execution & Risk Discipline
Rule-based entries and exits with a defined stop-loss on every position. Entry filters are intended to avoid wide-spread and thin-liquidity conditions, and every trade the model takes is logged and reviewable.
04
Continuous Model Evaluation
Walk-forward evaluation with regime detection across risk-on, risk-off and range-bound conditions, to reduce the risk of fitting the model to outdated market behaviour.

Risk Framing

A 29.67% maximum drawdown is a material risk characteristic of the model and should be considered alongside its historical returns. We publish drawdown information because performance should be evaluated on both return and risk, not returns alone — including when performance is unfavourable.

Risk controls are built into the strategy's rules: a stop-loss on every position, daily drawdown limits, exposure caps and reduced position sizing when volatility exceeds predefined thresholds.

Drawdown periods are an expected part of the return profile, not an anomaly. The live track record currently reflects a 60.89% historical win rate; this statistic may vary materially over time and does not predict future results. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
